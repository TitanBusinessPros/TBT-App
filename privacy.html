Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy describes how Titan Business Pros LLC handles information in the free tools collection at titanbusinesspros.github.io/TBT-App/. Linked tools and external websites may have different practices.

QR code information

Information you enter in this app's QR code maker is used in your browser to create the QR code. This website does not send your QR code entries to Titan Business Pros. If you choose Download, the image is saved on your device. If you choose Share, your device's sharing feature sends it to the recipient or app you select. Only enter information you are comfortable putting into a QR code that others may scan.

Contacting us

If you call, text, or email us using a link on this site, we receive the information you choose to provide and may use it to respond to your inquiry or provide requested services. Your phone, messaging, or email provider also handles that communication under its own privacy practices.

Hosting, images, and local storage

This app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub says it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. Some tool images are loaded from GitHub and may involve a separate request to GitHub. If you install or use the app offline, your browser may store site files in its cache or service worker storage. You can remove that stored data through your browser or device settings.

Analytics and other websites

The code for this app does not include its own analytics or advertising trackers. When you open a linked tool or another external site, that site may collect information and apply its own privacy policy. Review its policy before entering sensitive information there.

Questions and updates

For questions about information you have sent us, or to request access to or deletion of that information, contact titanbusinesspros@gmail.com or call 405-998-7979. We may update this policy by posting a new version here and changing the date above.
